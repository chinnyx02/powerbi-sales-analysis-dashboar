#  Sales Analysis Dashboard | Power BI

##  Project Status: Ongoing

This project focuses on the development of an interactive **Sales Analysis Dashboard using Microsoft Power BI**.

The objective is to transform raw sales data into meaningful business insights by analyzing revenue, costs, profit, sales volume, product performance, geographical performance, and monthly sales trends.

The dashboard is currently under development, with additional analysis, visual improvements, and business insights being added progressively.

---

##  Project Objectives

The key objectives of this project are to:

- Analyze overall sales performance.
- Monitor revenue, cost, and profitability.
- Identify top-performing shoe products.
- Analyze sales volume across different product categories.
- Compare revenue performance across countries.
- Identify monthly sales and profit trends.
- Develop interactive and user-friendly Power BI visualizations.
- Generate actionable insights to support data-driven decision-making.

##  Preliminary Insights

The analysis has currently identified the following findings:

### Product Performance

**Canvas** is currently the leading product in terms of total profit, generating approximately **27.8K**.

### Sales Volume

**Monk Straps** currently record the highest quantity sold, with approximately **575 units**.

### Geographical Performance

The **United States** currently records the highest revenue among the countries analyzed, generating approximately **39K**.

### Monthly Performance

The monthly trend indicates a noticeable increase in revenue and profit around **June**, followed by a decline toward **August**.

This trend requires further investigation to determine the factors contributing to the changes in performance.
